# TS-01 Team strategy philosophy

> SYNTHETIC DATA for retrieval testing.

## Core idea
Our team sails a **"consistent, conservative-percentage"** strategy. We do not try to win every race; we try to finish in the top third of every race and avoid big scores. In an Olympic regatta with one discard and a double-points medal race, a series of 4th–8th places beats a mix of wins and 20ths.

Our three rules:
1. **Speed first, strategy second, tactics third.** If we are not fast in the conditions, we sail in clear air in the middle of the course and keep options open.
2. **Commit only with evidence.** We go hard to one side only when at least two independent signals agree (e.g. venue history + today's compass log, or forecast + visible pressure on the water).
3. **Stay connected to the fleet.** Without strong evidence, we stay between the fleet and the favoured side and take small gains.

## Triggers
Applies every day. Condition-specific documents (TS-02 to TS-09) adjust this default.

## Start
- Default: start in the **favoured third of the line** with a clear lane, not at the extreme end.
- Only "win the end" when line bias is ≥ 10° or the favoured side is clear and strong (≥ 2 signals).
- Accept a second-row-free start over a risky pin or boat-end start.

## Upwind
- First beat: sail the **first 30% of the beat on the plan from the briefing**, then re-check pressure and shifts.
- Tack on headers of ≥ 5° (oscillating wind); ignore shifts < 5°.
- Avoid the laylines before the last 25% of the beat.

## Downwind
- Sail to pressure first, then angle. Gybe on lifts / headers ≥ 10°.
- Protect the inside at the leeward gate; choose the gate that leads to the favoured side of the next beat.

## Boat handling
- Settings and technique follow the class tuning guide. Change modes early, before conditions force it.

## Coach actions
- Morning briefing 08:00 with one clear game plan and one plan B.
- On the water before the warning signal: wind readings, line bias, current check (see TS-11).
- Between races: update the sailors with 3 facts only (side that paid, shift trend, any change in the forecast).

## Plan B
If the plan is clearly wrong after the first third of the beat (fleet on the other side gaining ≥ 5 boat lengths), **cut losses**: tack back toward the middle and re-join the fleet. No "hoping it comes back".

## Past lessons (synthetic)
- 2025 European Championship: 3 scores above 20th all came from going to an extreme side on one signal only. Led to rule 2.
- 2026 Test event: best series came from middle-right starts and consistent top-10 finishes.
