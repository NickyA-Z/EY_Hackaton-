# TS-10 Venue playbook – Port of LA (San Pedro)

> SYNTHETIC DATA for retrieval testing. Based loosely on public descriptions of the venue; numbers are invented for the hackathon.

## Typical day (July)
- **Morning (before 11:00):** light and variable, 3–8 kt. Use TS-04.
- **11:00–13:00:** sea breeze starts from the W–WSW. Use TS-07.
- **13:00–17:00:** 12–18 kt, sometimes 20+ kt late afternoon. Use TS-02 or TS-03.
- **Wind veers right** as it builds, about 5° per hour.
- **Ocean swell** about 0.6–1.2 m from the SW. Use TS-09 when ≥ 1 m.

## Known geographic patterns
- **Right side (north, toward the breakwater):** more pressure on most W–WSW days; favoured on the first beat after 13:00 in most past races.
- **Hills to the west:** with **SW wind**, wind shadow on the **left** of course A. Avoid the far left.
- **Inside the harbour:** wind 2–5 kt lighter than outside the breakwater.
- **Current:** weak (usually < 0.5 kt); flood tide sets toward the harbour mouth.

## Recommended default plan (W–WSW, 12–18 kt)
- Start near the committee boat end, tack onto port early, work the right.
- Watch for pressure differences across the course; do not go to the right layline too early on long beats.
- Downwind: right side for pressure and wave angle.

## When the playbook does NOT apply
- Wind from the S or E (rare): no reliable pattern; use TS-01.
- Sea breeze late (cloudy morning, marine layer): use TS-04 plan B.

## Past lessons (synthetic)
- 2026 test event: fleet launched on port to the right on most days, but pressure differences made laylines hard to judge; the boats that tacked back toward the middle in the last third gained.
