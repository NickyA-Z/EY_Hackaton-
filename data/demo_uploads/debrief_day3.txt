Day 3 debrief, ILCA 7, Outer Harbor.
Sea breeze filled from the SSW around 11:00 and kept veering right through the afternoon.
The right side paid on every first beat; boats that tacked early off the committee boat end gained most.
Pressure was stronger close to the breakwater, about 2 knots more than the middle.
Current was weak on the flood, slightly stronger near the harbour entrance.
We lost places downwind: too high on the runs, not enough waves used.
Setup: more vang and cunningham above 12 kt, ease outhaul in the chop.
Sailor felt fatigue in race 3; keep focus on hiking in the last beat.
